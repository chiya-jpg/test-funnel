Full-spectrum school health — not just band-aids.

What we deliver

Four layers of care — on one cadence.

Daily presence plus scheduled preventive rounds means things don't fall through the cracks.

Layer 1
Every day

Daily acute care

The everyday stuff a school nurse handles, now done well on-site — so parents aren't called for every low-grade fever.

  • Sick visits, fevers, headaches, stomach complaints
  • Wound care — cleaning, dressing, sutures triaged
  • Strep & flu rapid testing, URI assessment
  • Medication administration (PRN & scheduled)
  • Triage, parent notification, provider referral when needed
Layer 2
Monthly

Behavioral health rounds

Structured monthly reviews for kids on ADHD medication — so treatment decisions happen with a clinician, not in parking-lot conversations.

  • ADHD medication reviews with parent input
  • Height, weight, and side-effect tracking
  • Coordination with prescribing providers
  • Behavioral check-ins for identified students
Layer 3
Every 8 weeks

Chronic care management

Continuous support for the kids who need the most follow-through — so parents aren't the only ones tracking it.

  • Asthma action plans, inhaler technique, peak-flow trends
  • Allergy and EpiPen readiness
  • Diabetes monitoring in coordination with endocrinology
  • Care plan updates shared with parents and prescribers
Layer 4
Quarterly

Preventive screenings

The visits that get skipped when parents are busy — now delivered at school, documented, and sent home.

  • Sports participation clearances
  • Vision & hearing screens
  • BMI trending and obesity counseling
  • Immunization status review and reminder outreach
Parent portal

Parents see everything, in real time.

Every visit is logged, every note shared, every message delivered. No more "the nurse called, but I didn't catch what happened."
